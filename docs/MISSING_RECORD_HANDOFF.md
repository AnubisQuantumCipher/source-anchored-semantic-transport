## What remains unproved

This task explicitly requested a blocked response and stated the missing-record
boundary. It is not an unprompted discovery test or a compaction campaign. One
fixed-order pair supplies no statistical error-rate or latency claim. The prior
complete-record comparison also found no consequential-error advantage.

The three typed units are opaque whole-source propositions, marked `INCOMPLETE`.
The package retains all source bytes. Source wording supplies qualifications
not expressed independently by the short formula; empty uncertainty records do
not prove certainty. Interpretation entailment, completeness, source truth and
current authority remain unproved.

Observed call records establish neither operating-system isolation nor absence
of hidden access. Initial dispatch is encrypted in the available client record;
exact plaintext dispatch equality remains `UNKNOWN`. Model settings are recorded
settings, not independent backend attestation. The full campaign therefore keeps
its unknown validity limits alongside the narrower observed passes.

The local CLI reader was exercised through fresh agents in this environment.
This is not desktop-client acceptance, a cross-model result, x86_64 runtime
qualification or marketplace installation. No decoder, Ada/SPARK source, resource
bound or proof obligation changed in this checkpoint. Governing legal records
still need authentication, version control and a current-authority policy outside
these transport claims.

## Reproduction and retained failures

Follow the fixture's encoding and recovery commands and the
[reader guide](HANDOFF_READER.md) for deterministic controls. Freeze new input
identities, reader dependencies, live task and source-only rubric before any new
model trial. Keep evaluator material separate, retain the first submission and
actual final, then assess source fidelity and protocol independently. New model
answers require new evidence; they do not reproduce these observed answers by
definition.

Public artifacts contain synthetic source and answers, not private documents or
raw host logs. Complete host-specific launch envelopes and local reviews remain
retained locally and identified by hashes. A hash does not make unavailable
evidence independently inspectable or authenticate it.

without enforcing the review's exact file pins. The retained counterexample led
to explicit externally held review identity and full file coverage checks before
either recipient was dispatched. The trace collector was separately exercised
against missing, incomplete and multiple finals; it never substitutes the saved
submission for the actual final. Measurement-helper counterexamples and reviewer
checker mistakes are retained separately from repaired helpers and final evidence.
