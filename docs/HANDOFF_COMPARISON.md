# Matched source handoff: Cedar

Both first recipient answers passed the frozen source obligations in this one
synthetic comparison. The transport arm did **not** demonstrate fewer consequential
errors than the original-document arm. This is a bounded observation, not product
acceptance, a statistical result or proof of semantic completeness.

The [comparison receipt](../receipts/handoff-comparison-1.json) binds the reader
files, installed runtime, package, criteria, actual answers, controls and separate
assessments. The [host dependency supplement](../receipts/handoff-host-dependencies-1.json)
states what the delivery accounting excludes. Earlier receipts remain unchanged.

## Task and controls

The public [Cedar fixture](../examples/handoff-v1/README.md) contains an old approval,
a correction to an unfinished task, a revocation, scoped and conflicting reports,
missing acknowledgements and pending work. Recipients had to prepare a useful
local JSON draft, retain candidate scopes, preserve uncertainty and refrain from
treating stored records as current permission. They were forbidden to read the
unrelated source.

The original arm received the source files, a digest-bound neutral catalog and
bounded pages. It had no decoder or typed graph. The transport arm received the
matched share/ZIP and exact decoder, with focused retrieval and bounded pages.
Source bytes, live task, catalog order and resource limits matched. Transport
graph checks were explicitly inapplicable to the baseline; common behavioral
and source-retrieval duties remained binding.

The setup, first-access rules and source-only criteria were frozen before either
recipient. Original ran first, then transport, each requested with `fork_turns:
none`, `gpt-6.1-sol` and `high` reasoning. Available runtime records show those
model/effort settings. Each supplied one first candidate; neither was repaired,
retried or selected from multiple answers. Separate GPT-6.1 agents reviewed the
observable protocol and compared the actual final answers with the frozen source.

Predispatch evidence includes 13 reader controls, eight synthetic trace controls,
independent rehearsals, missing declared-context rejection, altered source/archive
rejection and a coherent replacement rejected by the separately held old package
identity. The coherent replacement succeeds only when deliberately given its new
identity in a separate positive control. No fallback reconstruction was accepted.

The transport arm made fewer calls and had a lower cumulative input observation
in this pair, while its largest observed context was higher. Framework/tool
prompts and repeated context are included. Cached inputs are components, not an
extra total to add. Overlapping cumulative records were not summed. These are not
tokenizations of the source or package, and the fixed-order pair does not establish
a general efficiency advantage. Monetary cost and energy were not measured.
Individual reported subprocess wall times are retained separately. The
[task-timing receipt](../receipts/handoff-timing-1.json) extracts client-reported
completion records from the unchanged traces. These are observations of this
fixed-order pair, not authenticated clocks or a controlled latency benchmark.

The original source contents total 3,701 bytes (JACKAL `exact`,
`parsed=402+483+454+2137+225`, `exact=3701`).
The complete transport share is 829 Unicode code points, 830 UTF-8 bytes, including
identity and reference. Its meaning ZIP is 84,840 bytes. This does not demonstrate
a 200-character post or a platform-specific counting rule. A straightforward
source-text/ID JSON envelope is 3,920 bytes; ordinary gzip of that envelope is
1,609 bytes, with byte recovery checked. Those simpler formats do not provide
equivalent typed readings, graph checks or decoder/protocol identity.

All copied handoff files are counted below, including duplicated task/interface,
manifest, launch records and templates. Shared setup includes reader helpers and,
for transport, the exact decoder. The host Python executable is separately pinned
and measured in the supplement, but already provided by the host and excluded
from these delivery figures. Complete OS, standard-library and native-library
installation costs remain unmeasured. Evaluation logs and generated answers are
separate retained evidence, not handoff input.

reconstruction remain the product goal; storage overhead is still material.

## Remaining limits and reproduction

Initial launch dispatch is encrypted in the available client record, so exact
plaintext dispatch equality remains `UNKNOWN`. Actual interface reads and
subsequent calls were checked. Recorded settings are not backend attestation,
and observed traces do not establish OS isolation or absence of hidden access.
The assessments therefore retain overall campaign validity as
`UNKNOWN_UNATTESTED_DISPATCH_HIDDEN_ACCESS_BACKEND_IDENTITY`, alongside their
narrower observed passes. Source truth, authentication, current authority and
whole-system semantics remain unproved.

The [local reader guide](HANDOFF_READER.md) gives the reproducible deterministic
control command, manifest and interfaces. The source fixture, criteria and exact
synthetic outputs are public. Host-specific prompts, raw client logs and full
local review archives are retained locally; their hashes do not make them publicly
inspectable. Repeating a model run creates a new candidate requiring new scoring.
No new native decoder or Ada/SPARK proof claim is introduced by this checkpoint.

Retained failures include the initial final-message collector missing the client's
`phase=final_answer` representation, preparation/measurement serializer errors,
an incorrect expected error string and a protocol-checker header mismatch. A
separately tested extraction supplement recovered the actual finals; the original
capture, failed helpers and candidate bytes were preserved. No product bounds,
source obligations or criteria were weakened.

The disconnected omission fixture has been encoded and byte-checked locally, but
its matched reader/model trial is still unrun. Native-compaction comparisons,
cross-model qualification, desktop-client acceptance, x86_64 runtime and marketplace
qualification also remain unrun. The next behavioral boundary is a separately
frozen missing-record comparison that must preserve `UNKNOWN` while still preparing
a useful blocked draft. This single successful pair does not resolve compaction
protection or establish a lower error rate.
